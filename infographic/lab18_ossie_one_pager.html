Two Real Rough Edges Where the Rosetta Stone Stops Today
Honest empirical findings from Snowflake 10.42.103
01 · Spec Drift Is Already Here 0.3.0.dev0 vs 0.1.1
The pitch of an open standard is one shared schema, yet upstream apache/ossie (0.3.0.dev0) has already moved ahead of Snowflake preview (0.1.1 wrapper, plain-string ai_context, expression.dialects). Teams still need a validator shim between spec and platform.
02 · Translation Stops at Platform Idioms CASE WHEN → SUM(SWITCH)
Conditional SQL SUM(CASE WHEN ...) exports to .pbit as SUM(SWITCH(...)), which breaks in Power BI because DAX SUM() requires a single physical column (it needs SUMX()). Keep portable models as star-schema base aggregates sliced by lookup dims.
KEY TAKEAWAY It's still early days — complex finance models aren't moving seamlessly across platforms yet, and YAML won't replace native ontologies or careful DAX. Still, seeing BI and AI agents pull from one spec beats three drifting copies.
